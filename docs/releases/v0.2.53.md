# Orca v0.2.53

Orca v0.2.53 completes the first runtime-owned Goal Mode loop that the TUI can
use end to end. Goal commands, durable state, continuation, cancellation,
terminal settlement, projection, and restart recovery now share one
`RuntimeHost` truth.

## What Changed

- TUI Goal set-and-run, resume, pause, and cancellation now use typed runtime
  surface commands instead of reconstructing lifecycle decisions in the
  renderer.
- Goal creation, run preparation, outer-turn settlement, verification, and
  continuation decisions retain exact SQLite mutation receipts and are
  committed with their typed surface events before they become visible.
- Runtime owner leases and operation/generation fences survive restart.
  Recovery replays pending Goal mutations, restores the in-flight outer turn,
  and never re-executes a successor that was already durably admitted.
- Turn status and end reason remain distinct through finalization.
  `MaxInnerTurns` is a resumable interruption; cost-budget exhaustion becomes a
  monetary budget terminal and pauses the Goal as `UsageLimit`.
- Structured plan changes count as substantive progress even without a new
  assistant message. Productive turns persist a progress barrier, while
  repeated identical gaps retain their streak across actor and process
  restarts.
- Goal finish retries preserve the actor-owned preview token until durable
  success. Their exact command digest binds usage, progress, verification,
  continuation identity, stop reason, and terminal semantics.

## Compatibility

No CLI flag, configuration, or SQLite schema migration is required. Existing
recorded sessions and Goal databases remain readable.

The milestone is intentionally scoped to ordinary turns and Goal Mode in the
TUI. Workflow/background surface convergence, ACP completion, and JSONL
compatibility work continue after this release.
